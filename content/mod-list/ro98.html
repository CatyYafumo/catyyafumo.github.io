Return of 98 (Ro98)

Return.


Main info

Return of 98 is a texture replacement mod for Touhou 6 EoSD - Embodiment of Scarlet Devil, it's development started since January, 2024 and it was finally released on February 27th, 2024 as a texture pack that transforms TH06 into a remix of the pc-98 era, which changes the UI, items, bullets, stats, stages, backgrounds, characters, etc.

This was one of the first released mods by caty-sumi, thinking on the pc-98 era enjoyer touhou fans.


Gameplay

You have Reimu Hakurei and Marisa Kirisame with their pc-98 outfits and apperance, you can choose between two shottypes for each one.

Across the stages, you will face a pc-98 character as a boss, there is at least one character from each pc-98 touhou game. It's specific list is:

•1 character from HRtP (without counting Reimu)

•1 character from SoEW (without counting Marisa)

•1 character from PoDD

•2 characters from LLS

•2 characters from MS

The rest of game mechanics are the same from the original game. So you can still using the same techniques you usually use.


Story

This story hasn't been registered into the game, but in resume:

A letter appeared for the shrine maiden, she read it and it was a very surprise: some familiar enemies demanded her to fight against them once more...


Music

Into this mod, the only tracks that wasn't changed are the title screen, endings and staff themes. All stages themes are the original version of a pc-98 theme, same for bosses until stage 3. Stage 4 bosses onwards have an EoSD version of their theme made by different compossers.

Trivia (MAY CONTAIN SPOILERS)

•This is the second mod from Caty-Sumi, but the first cronologically.

•All bosses have their original title, excepting the extra boss who's called "The one that shouldn't been forgotten".

•Along with THCaty, this is the only retexture that doesn't change any ending, but the only in which no spellcard was renamed.

•Originally, the extra boss had a pose similar to flandre in their sprite.

•This mod has an extra cover art inspired on Ultrakill, most exactly on the Ultrakill fan project: "Purgatorio: Indulgence" artwork.

•Unlike the rest of characters, who are faithful to their PC-98 design, stage 3 boss' design fuses parts from their Windows design.

•In JoMAD, Ro98 is represented with green and the symbol "98"
